## Story Missions 1-4 through 1-8

### 1-4 バナナは おやつに 入りますか？
- c1: common first explanation — a fixed price cap (e.g. 300 yen) limits field-trip snacks.
- c2: banana/whole fruit is commonly treated separately from the priced snack category.
- c3: what counts as おやつ commonly differs by class/school, not only by food type.
- c4 (root): the category boundary is a matter of convention, not inherent to the food.

### 1-5「みんなやってる」の「みんな」
- c1: children commonly say "everyone" has something when they want it.
- c2: checked closely, "みんな" commonly turns out to be a small specific group.
- c3: a strong personal want can make it feel like everyone has something.
- c4 (root): "みんな" commonly functions as a feeling-expression, not a literal headcount.

### 1-6「ずるい」ってなに？
- c1: a common first explanation for "ずるい" is someone got an advantage (e.g. chose first).
- c2: children also call it "ずるい" when someone broke the agreed rule, not only for luck.
- c3 (root): "ずるい" commonly means a shared agreed-upon expectation wasn't kept.

### 1-7「かして」と言われたら、かさなきゃいけない？
- c1: a common first explanation is that lending when asked is kind.
- c2: something precious is a commonly recognized exception to lending.
- c3 (root): whether to lend is commonly framed as the lender's own decision, not an absolute duty.

### 1-8 ランドセル 6年たっても、まだ同じ？
- c1: a common first explanation is that it looks the same overall despite changes.
- c2: more than half a well-used backpack's parts (strap, frame) are commonly repaired/replaced.
- c3: it's still called "the same" because the same person used it continuously.
- c4 (root): "staying the same" is commonly framed as decided by continuity of use, not materials.
